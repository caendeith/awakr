Stop a preview with the same button that started it.

* The preview on a sound row is now a toggle: tap it again to stop the looping
  sound, and the button turns into a stop square while it plays.
* While a preview is playing, the picker shows a stop bar under its title, so
  there is always a way out without hunting for the row.
* Preview in the alarm editor toggles too, and closing the picker or opening it
  from the editor silences whatever was playing.
